## 🙋 About me

- 🎮 I hang around **Monika After Story** and **Super Mario 64 Coop**.
- 🧩 I make mods because I like adding that extra touch games were missing.
- 🌍 I believe games should be enjoyed in your own language, so I translate into **Spanish, Catalan, Galician and Basque**.
- 💬 *If I like a game, I look for ways to improve it; if I love it, I share it.*

## 🛠️ Featured projects

### 💚 [Voy a jugar un juego específico · MAS Submod](https://github.com/gg21kiy/Voy-a-jugar-un-juego-especifico-MAS-Submod)
A submod for **Monika After Story** that adds dialogue options to tell Monika which game you're about to play. Compatible with *Submod Updater Plugin*.

`Ren'Py`

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gg21kiy/gg21kiy/main/Screenshots/VAJAUJEMS/p1.jpg" width="600" alt="MAS Submod: Monika asks for the game's name" />
</p>

📥 **Installation:** download the latest version from [Releases](https://github.com/gg21kiy/Voy-a-jugar-un-juego-especifico-MAS-Submod/releases) and copy the folder into `game/Submods/` in your Monika After Story install.
